All scalebars were calibrated using ImageJ and the following magnification-scale calibration image: https://github.com/akashmitraa/youtube-resources/blob/6df67eccb04152d1c5fb97a009535d9a614a534c/allscales.gif, under the assumption that all images were taken at 40x. 20 um scalebars are provided for all images on the bottom right, except for the BCNB dataset, where we provide a 200 um scalebar at the top right of the image (given the image size)
